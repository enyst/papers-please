- **One-line take:** Pins down "emergence" rigorously (from complexity science) before asking whether LLMs have it — and separates two things people conflate: emergent *capabilities* ("More is Different") vs emergent *intelligence* ("Less is More", i.e. cheaper/faster reuse of capabilities to solve problems).

- **The two slogans as a real distinction:**
  - **More is Different** — many-body systems manifest higher-level properties describable by lower-dimensional effective variables (coarse-graining). This is the legitimate, measurable sense of emergence.
  - **Less is More** — intelligence as the *increasingly efficient* use of those emergent capabilities. Intelligence isn't just having capabilities; it's compressing/reusing them cheaply.

- **On the "emergent abilities of LLMs" debate:** reviews approaches to *quantifying* emergence (rather than taking benchmark step-changes at face value). Nods to the known critique that some "emergence" is an artifact of discontinuous metrics — so it insists on measurement before metaphysics.

- **The verdict it builds toward:** LLMs plausibly show emergent *capabilities* in the coarse-graining sense, but emergent *intelligence* (the Less-is-More efficiency) is the higher bar and not established. Emergence ≠ intelligence.

- **Why it matters for us:** the "Less is More" framing is a genuinely useful lens for agents — an agent that solves a task with less context, fewer tool calls, cheaper computation is exhibiting the efficiency signature of intelligence, not just capability. It also gives a disciplined way to talk about "emergence" instead of hype.

- **Abstract:** Emergence describes how many-body systems manifest novel higher-level properties describable by lower-dimensional effective variables — 'More is Different'. Intelligence is an emergent property manifesting increasingly efficient uses of emergent capabilities to solve problems — 'Less is More'. The paper examines claims that LLMs exhibit emergent capabilities, reviews approaches to quantifying emergence, and asks whether LLMs possess emergent intelligence.
